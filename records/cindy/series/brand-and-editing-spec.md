# Brand & editing spec — "Cindy's tennis diary" short-video series

The short videos are not standalone clips: they are **episodes of one long-running log**.
Same identity, same layout, same tone every episode — only the footage and the one-line copy change,
so a viewer recognises the series immediately.

## Identity

| Item | Value | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Series / account name | **Cindy's tennis diary** | first name only; no surname, school or club |
| Standing line | **比分会过去，习惯会留下** ("scores pass, habits stay") | carried over from the family's earlier videos, for continuity |
| Episode number | `No.01`, incrementing once per episode | single running registry |
| Palette | accent `#F05A37` + deep ink `#0E1116` | only these two, series-wide |
| Type | Hiragino Sans GB / PingFang SC, bold, white with black outline | matches the earlier episodes |
| Watermark | small top-left mark on every shot | on title cards the orange chip replaces it (never both) |

## Fixed structure of every episode

| Section | Length | Content | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hook | 3.2 s | Big line `tennis diary · day N` + "one thing today: X" | the first three seconds decide retention: one hook for the long run, one for today |
| 2. Body | 8 shots × 2.5–3.5 s | real hitting only; the key stroke replayed at 0.45×; one caption per shot | density and rhythm |
| 3. Fixed ender | 4.6 s | brand mark + standing line + one line about what she learned + next-episode teaser + follow prompt + number stamp | the same ending every episode is the memory anchor |

- **N is real**: counted from the first written training record, so it grows with the archive
  (150 days at the time of writing). The asset is not one video but a visible run of them.
- Caption style: one idea per shot, **keyword in the accent colour and 10 % larger**.
- Editing rules: all shots from the same session (same outfit, same camera distance); cuts land on
  the swing; 0.35 s cross-dissolves between shots; all text inside the top 80 % of the frame.
- Every episode exports a `cover.jpg` (hook card) and `endcard.jpg` (fixed ender) for upload.

## Publishing kit (template)

- Title: `tennis diary · day N | <the one thing practised today>`
- Copy: three to five restrained lines; ends with the standing line.
- Hashtags: `#少儿网球 #网球女孩 #青少年网球 #网球训练 #成长记录`
- Music: the file ships with a silent track; the track is chosen inside the app at upload time.
- Pinned comment: one quiet line about the process, never about the result.

## Rules

1. **Process only.** No scores, no rankings, no win/loss in any caption; the numbers used are
   record-keeping numbers (days, sessions, clips, reports).
2. **No identifying detail** — no surname, school, club or medical information in public material.
3. **Drafts only.** The pipeline never publishes; publishing, titles and music are a human decision.
4. One idea per caption; no clickbait phrasing.

## Relationship to this repository

An episode number corresponds to a dated entry in the monthly records, so the video series and the
written log stay in step: one video = one record = one traceable step of a long progression.
